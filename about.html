About LOVA

Support that feels
private, human, and familiar.

LOVA is a private SRHR and wellness companion for adults across Africa, helping users learn, track, ask, share, and care with confidence.

Rooted in African realities, cultures, privacy needs, care gaps, relationships, and everyday wellness experiences — LOVA supports adults who want to learn without shame, ask without fear, track without confusion, and care for themselves with more confidence.

Our story

It began with a
simple truth.

People have questions about their bodies, relationships, choices, care, and wellbeing — but many do not have a safe place to ask.

Across many communities, sexual and reproductive health is surrounded by silence, stigma, myths, and discomfort. At the same time, people are navigating real life: relationships, privacy, culture, money, pressure, pregnancy, body changes, and emotional wellbeing.

A way that feels private.

A way that feels human.

A way that respects culture without letting shame block care.

LOVA brings together learning, period and pregnancy tracking, anonymous community, Kungwi guidance, expert-informed support, and wellness care in one platform designed around dignity, privacy, and trust.

Mission

To simplify access to trusted and relevant sexual and reproductive health information, tools, community support, and care for adults across Africa.

Vision

A future where every person can understand their body, make informed choices, access support, and speak about sexual and reproductive wellbeing without shame.

What we believe

Principles that shape
everything we build.

01

SRHR education should be clear

People should not need complicated medical language to understand their own bodies.

02

Context matters

Health decisions are shaped by culture, relationships, privacy, access, emotions, beliefs, and everyday realities.

03

Privacy is care

Sensitive questions deserve safe, discreet, and respectful spaces.

04

SRH Wellness should include dignity

No one should feel ashamed for wanting to learn, prepare, protect themselves, or seek support.

05

Men belong in the conversation

Healthier relationships and communities require informed women, men, partners, and peers.

06

African realities deserve thoughtful solutions

LOVA is designed with African cultures, languages, relationships, access gaps, myths, and lived experiences in mind.

07

Traditional wisdom can meet modern care

LOVA recognizes culturally familiar guidance, including Kungwi support, while still encouraging professional medical care.

08

Digital support should lead to better care

Technology should help people learn, prepare, and seek the right support — not replace professional care.
