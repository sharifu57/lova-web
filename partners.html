LOVA works with partners who believe adults and young people deserve better access to trusted, stigma-free contextual SRHR information, wellness tools, care products, and support.

Rooted in African realities, LOVA collaborates with institutions, universities, NGOs, development partners, health providers, brands, creators, and community organizations to improve awareness, digital engagement, referral pathways, and wellbeing outcomes.

Why partner with LOVA

A digital platform for
healthier conversations.

LOVA sits at the intersection of contextual SRHR education, digital health, anonymous community, youth engagement, period and pregnancy tracking, Kungwi guidance, wellness care, and demand generation.

For users,

LOVA is a private companion.

For partners,

Partnership opportunities

Many ways to work
together.

Universities & Colleges

Support student wellbeing through contextual SRHR education, anonymous Q&A, digital campaigns, peer engagement, and awareness activities.

NGOs & Development Partners

Support SRHR programming, youth engagement, gender equity, menstrual health, contraception awareness, and referral pathways.

Health Providers

Connect users to trusted care, professional guidance, testing, contraception services, antenatal support, and appropriate referrals.

Brands & Product Partners

Reach wellness-conscious users through selected products, education, care bundles, campaigns, and discreet commerce.

Creators & Educators

Collaborate on youth-friendly SRHR content, storytelling, short videos, explainers, campaigns, and community conversations.

Kungwi & Cultural Guides

Bring culturally familiar guidance into a safer, modern, digital space for adults seeking support around relationships, intimacy, and care.

Employers & Institutions

Support staff and community wellbeing with private access to SRHR education and wellness resources.
