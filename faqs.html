Contextual SRHR means sexual and reproductive health understood in real life context. It includes the body, relationships, culture, privacy, myths, emotions, access to care, protection, consent, personal care, and the everyday decisions people make.
Kungwi guidance refers to culturally familiar support from traditional guides who understand relationships, intimacy, preparation, body confidence, care, and sensitive life transitions. On LOVA, this guidance is brought into a modern, private, and safer digital space.
