About LOVA

Support that feels
private, human, and familiar.

LOVA is a private SRHR and wellness companion for adults across Africa, helping users learn, track, ask, share, and care with confidence.

Rooted in African realities, cultures, privacy needs, care gaps, relationships, and everyday wellness experiences — LOVA supports adults who want to learn without shame, ask without fear, track without confusion, and care for themselves with more confidence.

Our story

It began with a
simple truth.

People have questions about their bodies, relationships, choices, care, and wellbeing — but many do not have a safe place to ask.

Across many communities, sexual and reproductive health is surrounded by silence, stigma, myths, and discomfort. At the same time, people are navigating real life: relationships, privacy, culture, money, pressure, pregnancy, body changes, and emotional wellbeing.

A way that feels private.

A way that feels human.

A way that respects culture without letting shame block care.

LOVA brings together learning, period and pregnancy tracking, anonymous community, Kungwi guidance, expert-informed support, and wellness care in one platform designed around dignity, privacy, and trust.

Mission

To simplify access to trusted and relevant sexual and reproductive health information, tools, community support, and care for adults across Africa.

Vision

A future where every person can understand their body, make informed choices, access support, and speak about sexual and reproductive wellbeing without shame.

Fertile window

Helpful estimates,
not guarantees.

LOVA can estimate your fertile window using the cycle information you provide. These estimates can help you understand your body better — but they should not be used as your only method of contraception.

For pregnancy prevention, speak to a qualified health provider about reliable contraception options.

Important note

LOVA tracking tools provide estimates and educational support. They are not medical diagnosis, treatment, antenatal care, or emergency care.

Cycle and pregnancy-related information may not be accurate for everyone — especially when cycles are irregular, postpartum, affected by stress, influenced by hormonal contraception, or changed by health conditions.

Our privacy promise

We collect only what
is needed.

LOVA is designed to support private learning, period and pregnancy tracking, wellness notes, anonymous community participation, culturally aware guidance, and care access.

We aim to collect only what is needed to provide the service, improve the experience, protect users, and meet legal requirements. We do not want users to feel exposed, judged, or unsafe when using LOVA.

What we treat with care

Personal questions Period tracking Pregnancy information Cycle & wellness notes Symptoms & mood logs Community participation Sensitive SRHR interests Support conversations Kungwi guidance requests Account information Care preferences Reports & concerns

Anonymous does not mean unsafe

In Unfiltered, users can participate without displaying their real name. This helps people ask and share more safely. However, anonymity does not allow harm — LOVA may review, moderate, restrict, or remove content that violates community rules or creates safety risks.

Cultural guidance and professional care both matter

LOVA may offer access to Kungwi guidance, trained guides, SRHR educators, or verified professionals — meant to provide culturally aware education, practical guidance, and emotional reassurance. Kungwi guidance does not replace medical diagnosis, treatment, antenatal care, emergency care, or professional mental health support.

Education is support. It is not a diagnosis.

LOVA provides educational information, wellness tools, tracking support, and guidance. It does not provide emergency care, medical diagnosis, treatment, or in-person clinical services. Users should seek qualified medical care for urgent symptoms, pregnancy concerns, infections, severe pain, or mental health emergencies.

Built for adults 18+

LOVA is designed for adults aged 18 and above. Users must follow the age requirements stated in the app, website, Terms of Use, and applicable laws.
