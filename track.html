Fertile window

Helpful estimates,
not guarantees.

LOVA can estimate your fertile window using the cycle information you provide. These estimates can help you understand your body better — but they should not be used as your only method of contraception.

For pregnancy prevention, speak to a qualified health provider about reliable contraception options.

Important note

LOVA tracking tools provide estimates and educational support. They are not medical diagnosis, treatment, antenatal care, or emergency care.

Cycle and pregnancy-related information may not be accurate for everyone — especially when cycles are irregular, postpartum, affected by stress, influenced by hormonal contraception, or changed by health conditions.
