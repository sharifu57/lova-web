Wellness support should feel safe, not scary.

Many people grow up with questions about their bodies, periods, pregnancy, protection, consent, relationships, infections, and emotions — but not everyone has a safe place to ask. Some questions feel too personal. Some are surrounded by myths, silence, or fear.

LOVA exists to change that.

What LOVA does

One private space for your
whole wellness journey.

LOVA brings together the tools, information, community, and care support people need to make informed decisions about their bodies, relationships, and wellbeing.

Learn

Simple, trusted content on periods, pregnancy, contraception, consent, relationships, body changes, emotional wellbeing, and everyday wellness.

Track

Period and pregnancy tracking, symptom logging, mood notes, energy patterns, reminders, and wellness insights to understand your body over time.

Ask

Private question support for sensitive topics people may not feel comfortable asking out loud — answered with care, never judgment.

Share

Anonymous conversations through Unfiltered — a space for real questions, real stories, polls, and peer support.

Kungwi Guidance

Culturally aware guidance from traditional SRHR and relationship guides — relatable, real-life support for body confidence, intimacy, and life transitions.

Care

Access selected wellness, period care, pregnancy support, protection, and personal care products — discreetly and supportively.

Why LOVA exists

Because silence should not be the default.

Sexual and reproductive health is part of everyday life, yet it is often treated as something people should hide, fear, or figure out alone. LOVA helps close that gap by giving users a trusted starting point.

We believe people deserve support that is clear, practical, culturally aware, emotionally intelligent, and non-judgmental — whether someone is tracking a period, following a pregnancy journey, learning about contraception, or preparing for intimacy.
